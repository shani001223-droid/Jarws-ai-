# Harmonic and geometric patterns: measure the legs before naming the structure

Reviewed **28 October 2026, Asia/Karachi**. Original educational analysis with
limited attributed descriptions of public frameworks. Historical examples use
**Binance Spot BTCUSDT, USDT per BTC, daily UTC bars**. No proprietary books,
course charts or commercial pattern software are reproduced or configured.
The offline diagnostic is not a complete harmonic recognizer or runtime signal.

## What is it?

Harmonic-pattern frameworks describe ordered price legs using retracement,
extension and symmetry constraints. A common notation is `X,A,B,C,D`, but not
every named framework uses identical anchor meanings. A recognizer needs more
than five visually attractive points: it needs a causal anchor rule, ordering,
leg definitions, accepted ratio ranges, tolerance, invalidation and availability
times. A ratio resemblance is not proof of a reversal or calibrated confidence.

Define lengths without ambiguity. For a five-point sequence, let
`XA=abs(A-X)`, `AB=abs(B-A)`, `BC=abs(C-B)` and `CD=abs(D-C)`. Useful diagnostics
include `AB/XA`, `BC/AB`, `CD/BC`, `AD/XA` and `CD/AB`, where `AD=abs(D-A)`.
Absolute lengths alone do not verify that legs alternate or that points lie
inside the required parent segment. Check direction and location separately.
Zero-length denominators are invalid measurements, not ratios to replace with
zero or a large arbitrary number.

Retracement origin is crucial. In an **invented arithmetic illustration**, X
is 100 and A is 200, so XA is 100. A downward 0.786 retracement from A is
`200-0.786*100=121.4`, not `100+0.786*100=178.6`. Those are different prices.
Similarly, projecting AB from C means adding or subtracting the measured AB
length according to the leg direction; it does not mean measuring everything
from X. These invented numbers demonstrate algebra, not a historical setup.

Fibonacci-derived chart labels mix mathematical approximations and practitioner
conventions. Ratios near 0.618 and 1.618 relate to the limiting ratio of
successive Fibonacci numbers; other commonly used labels arise through roots,
reciprocals or selected conventions. Mathematical relationships do not imply
that traded prices must stop there. A 0.50 retracement can be a defined chart
level without being the limiting Fibonacci ratio.

### Public framework distinctions

**AB=CD and alternate forms:** the basic construction compares two same-direction
legs separated by a retracement. The public creator description discusses
near-equal legs and convergence with a BC projection; alternate versions use
other fixed AB multiples. A projected D is a candidate price, not an observed
turn. Choosing an alternate only after the original projection fails is a new
hypothesis and must not erase the original failure.

**Gartley:** the checked creator framework emphasizes B near a 0.618 XA
retracement, D near a 0.786 XA retracement, and additional AB=CD/BC convergence.
Those are necessary relationships within that framework, not a license to
declare a pattern from one matching number. A mirrored orientation also needs
correct point ordering and location, rather than simply changing a direction
label on the same coordinates.

**Bat and alternate Bat:** the public Bat description separates it from a
Gartley by a shallower B and a deeper D reference near 0.886 XA, with additional
leg constraints. Its alternate description discusses a different shallow-B
alignment and a completion beyond X near a 1.13 projection. Treat these as
distinct versions. A stop-out in one version cannot be retrospectively
renamed as a successful entry in another.

**Butterfly:** the checked ideal framework uses a B retracement near 0.786 XA
and an extension reference near 1.27 XA, alongside AB=CD/BC relationships.
Several passages on the creator page describe different BC alignments, so
HOSHI must cite the selected variant rather than silently combine all of them.
The description is an attributed identification framework, not independent
evidence of reliable returns.

**Crab and Deep Crab:** the checked pages emphasize an XA extension near 1.618.
The dedicated Deep Crab description distinguishes a much deeper B retracement
near 0.886 from the regular family's shallower B range. The Crab page itself
contains inconsistent discovery-year statements and a later paragraph resembling
the deep variant. This chapter assigns no single invented discovery year and
does not use that inconsistent paragraph to define an executable classifier.

**Shark and 5-0:** these have their own extended-segment and reciprocal
relationships and should not be forced into an arbitrary five-pivot template.
The creator's Shark description distinguishes it from ordinary M/W forms; the
5-0 description identifies a 50% BC retracement and additional reciprocal/leg
conditions. A shared ratio label does not make their anchors or management
rules interchangeable. Their promotional accuracy claims are not adopted.

## Why does it form?

An observed path contains many possible segments, ratios and repeated
proportions. Selecting only attractive anchor combinations after the event can
make familiar numbers appear persuasive. The existence of a numerical
relationship is an observation; whether that relationship changes future
outcomes after costs is a different empirical question. Neither the ratio nor
the name reveals who traded or why.

Geometry is sensitive to sampling and pivot rules. A daily wick extreme can
belong to a different bar than the corresponding closing extreme. A
percentage-reversal ZigZag can revise its latest endpoint, while a fixed
two-right-bar pivot has an explicit delay. Those constructions cannot share
an entry timestamp merely because their hindsight drawings look similar.

Many ratios are algebraically dependent because they reuse the same leg
lengths. Several projections near one price therefore need not be independent
evidence. For example, measuring a retracement and its reciprocal describes
the same underlying numbers in two ways. A confluence count should not award
independent statistical certainty simply for displaying more related labels.

## Application zone

First define anchors and the time each becomes eligible. The examples here use
**hoshi-five-pivot-ratios-1**: five consecutive, strictly time-ordered,
alternating two-left/two-right wick pivots. Same-bar high/low ambiguity is
rejected. Skipping an inconvenient pivot is not permitted within this version.
All five points must be confirmed by the second following close of D. This
is a narrow research convention, not a replacement for every creator's swing
selection or a claim of source-method parity.

Second compute raw ratios and location checks before naming anything. An
attractive `AB/XA` value cannot compensate for a C outside the permitted parent
leg or a D beyond a framework's allowed boundary. Report each observation
individually. If required points or a primary rule specification are unavailable,
the named pattern remains unverified rather than being filled in from memory.

The diagnostic compares only two necessary Gartley relationships with an
explicit exploratory tolerance of **±0.03 absolute ratio units**. This is not
±3% of price, three ticks, or an independently optimized threshold. Passing
one or both diagnostics is insufficient for full validation. Consequently
`pattern_validated=false` and `win_probability=null` remain explicit in the
receipt. No probability is calculated from distance to a target ratio.

Third separate projection time, contact time, pivot confirmation and possible
execution. Once X through C are known, a model can project a candidate region,
subject to its rules. It cannot call D a confirmed turn before subsequent
confirmation exists. An order policy based on projected contact and one based
on a confirmed D are different studies with different prices and risks.
Comparing them fairly requires distinct records and actual execution assumptions.

Define a projected zone using declared tolerances and a fixed intersection or
aggregation rule. If the projections disagree, retain the disagreement. Do not
widen the zone until a future low fits, replace a failed ratio with another
family, or move X after the candidate fails. Invalidation and expiry belong
in the original candidate record. Any later family conversion needs a new
record with its own knowledge time.

Treat scale carefully. Price ratios are invariant to multiplying every price
by the same positive constant, but changing the chart transform or mixing
adjusted and raw prices can change visual and numerical relationships. Time
symmetry is another feature with a separate unit; it must not be inferred from
price-length equality. Session gaps, contract rolls and venue differences also
need explicit treatment before transferring a framework to another market.

Finally require independent evidence for performance. A sourced definition is
not validation of an entry model. Creator descriptions and promotional claims
are not calibrated HOSHI probabilities. Genuine chronological evaluation must
include rejected candidates, failed projections, observation delays, costs,
drawdowns and ambiguity. The current chapter provides measurements and
counterexamples, not that evaluation or a deployed harmonic strategy.

## Failure zones

**One-ratio attraction:** one measured leg can lie close to a familiar number
while the overall geometry violates necessary conditions. The June 2020 case
below shows this directly. Several visually plausible turns do not overcome
the contradictory measured relationships.

**Hindsight endpoint selection:** a pattern finder can improve apparent fit by
choosing among many X/A/B/C/D combinations. That search is a source of model
selection, not free evidence. Record all searched variants and preserve the
declared consecutive-pivot constraint. A pattern that requires future candles
to identify D cannot justify an entry at D's earlier wick using that later
confirmation.

**Unbounded family switching:** a failed Gartley may be relabeled a Bat, then
an alternate extension after another price move. These are distinct hypotheses,
not proof the original prediction was correct. Keep cancellations and earlier
losses; do not retain only the eventual reversal that looks most convincing.

**Incomplete or conflicting specifications:** publicly described variants can
disagree, and a complete proprietary execution method may be unavailable.
Record exactly which relationships were checked and which were not. This
chapter paraphrases limited public identification descriptions and uses
original arithmetic; it does not claim possession of licensed course materials,
full creator-method certification or every unpublished variant.

**Precision mistaken for certainty:** three decimal places do not create an
edge. A narrow zone can be crossed abruptly, a projected level might never
trade on the supported venue, and costs can dominate a small reaction. No
harmonic pattern has a 100% application zone. Pattern-distance scores remain
descriptive until any separate probability model is independently calibrated.

## Three real historical examples with dates

The companion pattern JSON links exact points to captured raw source hashes.
Point dates refer to daily UTC bars, ending **23:59:59.999 UTC**. These are
selected retrospective diagnostics; all three remain **unvalidated patterns**.
No profitable historical harmonic setup is invented to satisfy an example quota.

### Example 1 — 15–29 June 2020: one close ratio alongside incompatible geometry

The consecutive alternating points were X **8,910.45 on June 15**, A
**9,589.00 on June 16**, B **9,170.95 on June 20**, C **9,780.00 on June 22**
and D **8,833.00 on June 27**. D was first confirmed at the **June 29** close.
`AB/XA` was approximately **0.61609**, near the diagnostic target. However,
`AD/XA` was approximately **1.11414**, and `BC/AB` approximately **1.45688**.

C exceeded A and D fell below X. Those locations and ratios do not establish
the described Gartley geometry. The near match of one leg must not become
a high-confidence long. The captured source ends June 30; this chapter makes
no five-bar-forward claim beyond that boundary and supplies no invented fill
at D's low.

### Example 2 — 5–27 March 2021: a visually ordered sequence outside the selected ratio limits

The points were X **46,300.00 on March 5**, A **61,844.00 on March 13**, B
**53,271.34 on March 16**, C **60,129.97 on March 18** and D **50,427.56 on
March 25**. The complete sequence became eligible at the **March 27** close.
Its measured ratios included `AB/XA≈0.55151`, `BC/AB≈0.80006`,
`CD/BC≈1.41463` and `AD/XA≈0.73446`.

Both necessary-ratio diagnostics failed the stated tolerance. A zigzag-looking
shape and a plausible retracement narrative are insufficient to override the
measurements. No post hoc tolerance widening, alternative anchor selection or
trade result is claimed. Another framework would need its own explicitly
specified and separately tested predicate.

### Example 3 — 26 May–9 June 2022: measured retracements without a certified match

The consecutive points were X **28,019.56 on May 26**, A **32,399.00 on
May 31**, B **29,282.36 on June 3**, C **31,765.64 on June 6** and D
**29,218.96 on June 7**. D's confirmation completed at the **June 9** close.
The ratios were `AB/XA≈0.71165`, `BC/AB≈0.79678`, `CD/BC≈1.02553`,
`AD/XA≈0.72613` and `CD/AB≈0.81712`.

The two necessary-ratio diagnostics again failed. The fact that some other
ratio looks familiar cannot convert the sequence into a validated named
pattern. The receipt retains raw observations, false validation status and
null winning probability. It does not forecast the next move from a historical
analogy or imply a strategy was tested on this case.

## How to use it to HUNT a trade

HUNT starts with a paper research specification, a supported actual feed and
fully declared anchor/ratio rules. Identify what is observed and what is merely
projected. Missing points, ambiguous same-bar turns, unsupported markets and
stale required data produce WAIT. Historical examples are not today's quotes.

Compute all necessary constraints from the permitted prefix and retain failed
ones. A projected zone may become a candidate only under the declared version;
it remains separate from a confirmed turn and an executable trigger. Freeze
expiry, invalidation and entry policy before inspecting later price action.
Do not turn a ratio-distance diagnostic into a winning-probability percentage.

If a full paper execution model is later available, size only within bounded
cash risk using actual observed distances and realistic costs. Preserve
zero-entry, cancelled and ambiguous cases. Evaluate chronological independent
samples and export source hashes, points, times, tolerances and all failures.
Default output can be `WAIT — aik ratio milta hai, magar baqi zaroori geometry
match nahi karti.` Explain the detailed framework only when asked.
