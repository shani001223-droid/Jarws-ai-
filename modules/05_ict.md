# ICT: Killzones, Silver Bullet and Power of Three

Knowledge version: `hoshi-trading-knowledge-v2`. Language: English definitions with Roman Urdu-ready explanations.

## Evidence boundary

Educational knowledge, not a signal or a win-probability model. Prices, stops,
position sizes and outcomes must not be invented. Each link below opens a live
chart for inspection; the conditional walkthrough is **not a verified live
pattern detection**, replay result, or recommendation. Record venue, symbol,
timeframe, closed-candle UTC timestamps, timezone and source retrieval time
before calling an observation current. Reject missing/stale candles. Multiple
features derived from one OHLCV history are correlated evidence.

## Definition

ICT names a discretionary methodology associated with Michael Huddleston.
Hoshi uses explicitly versioned research windows in America/New_York: London
02:00-05:00, New York 07:00-10:00, Silver Bullet AM 10:00-11:00 and PM
14:00-15:00 (half-open intervals, weekdays). These are HOSHI configuration choices,
not a claim that all ICT curricula use identical hours. Power of Three describes
an accumulation -> false break/manipulation hypothesis -> distribution narrative.

## Application Zone

Convert aware UTC timestamps through IANA America/New_York; never hardcode
a year-round UTC offset. Build an overnight/Asian range using a declared window,
then observe a possible sweep and displacement. A Silver Bullet hour is a time
filter only; still require HTF, target, location and an actual later FVG retest.
Preserve the 24/7 nature of crypto and disclose the weekday restriction.

## Failure Zone

No sweep or displacement means the narrative is incomplete. Macro news can
widen spread and invalidate a time pattern. DST transitions, holidays and differing
London/US changeover weeks defeat fixed PKT offsets. Hoshi has no live calendar
feed; time eligibility is not news clearance. PO3 phases are not known in advance.
